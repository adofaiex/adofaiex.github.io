# About ADOFAI Expansion

**ADOFAI Expansion** is an organization dedicated to researching and expanding the functions of _A Dance of Fire and Ice_ (ADOFAI).

> An organization that researches and expands the functions of ADOFAI.

Interesting repositories about ADOFAI live here, including but not limited to **Mod, Patch, Shader, Tool**.

This is a very casual organization with **no organizational rules**.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/adofaiex>
- Website / Mod platform: <https://modrift.org>
- Discord: <https://discord.gg/ddndY4xXeK>

## What we do

- **Mod** —— game mods for fixing, optimizing, visual effects and utilities
- **Patch** —— enhancements and fixes for the game
- **Shader** —— research and implementation of visual effects
- **Tool** —— chart and level related tools and libraries
